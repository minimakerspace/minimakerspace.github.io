# Welcome to the workbench

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These docs are under active construction. Pages may move while we turn workshop notes into practical guides.

mini MAKERSPACE Docs is where we document the decisions behind our 3D printing, electronics, and rapid prototyping work. The goal is simple: explain enough of the process that another maker can understand it, reproduce it, and improve it.

## What you will find here

- **Build guides** with tools, materials, settings, and step-by-step checks.
- **Field notes** that record what worked, what failed, and what we changed.
- **Reference pages** for recurring details such as tolerances, connectors, and materials.
- **Project notes** that connect mechanical, electronic, and fabrication decisions.

## A note on safety

Documentation cannot replace good judgment. Check ratings, datasheets, ventilation needs, and tool-specific instructions before building. When a project involves mains voltage, heat, pressure, chemicals, or moving machinery, get qualified help.

## Need a prototype built?

The documentation is for learning. For design, fabrication, or electronics support, visit [minimakerspace.id](https://minimakerspace.id).
