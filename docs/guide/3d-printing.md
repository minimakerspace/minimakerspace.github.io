# 3D Printing

This section will cover practical FDM printing: preparing models, choosing materials, tuning slicer settings, checking tolerances, and diagnosing failures.

> [!TIP]
> A successful print begins with the part's job, not the slicer. Decide what the part must fit, carry, resist, or look like before choosing settings.

## Before you slice

1. Confirm the model uses the intended units.
2. Check that walls and small features are printable with your nozzle size.
3. Choose an orientation based on strength, support, surface quality, and tolerance.
4. Verify clearances for every mating part.
5. Decide whether the print is a visual model, fit test, or functional part.

## Material quick guide

| Material | Useful for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PLA | Visual models, jigs, low-heat indoor parts | Softens with heat; can be brittle |
| PETG | Functional parts, brackets, light outdoor use | Stringing and bridging need tuning |
| TPU | Flexible feet, bumpers, strain relief | Slow printing and constrained filament paths |
| ABS/ASA | Heat resistance and tougher enclosures | Warping, fumes, and enclosure requirements |
